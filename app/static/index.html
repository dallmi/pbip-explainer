        Why this helps

        Semantic models grow for years. The people who built them move on, and nobody is sure how the numbers are made.

        Problem 1
        Nobody knows what a number means

        Measures are named, not explained. Users guess, and the guesses differ.

        Fix: a data glossary and every measure in plain words, next to its DAX.

        Problem 2
        Changes break things silently

        Renaming a column or a measure can break visuals on pages nobody checks.

        Fix: lineage from source to visual and an impact check for every object.

        Problem 3
        The model drifts

        Bi-directional filters, unused measures and implicit aggregations pile up.

        Fix: a health check with concrete findings and advice.

        Ready when You see the report's pages, measures and data model in the Documentation tab, and the HTML or Markdown export opens on its own.
